# Scientific Content Review — I18

Reviewed 9 November 2026 against repository baseline `d12bc0b`. **Report only: no application, scientific data, migration, account, or production changes are made by this task.** This is a source-based technical review, not academic approval, a supplier SDS, a safe practical recipe, or institutional safety certification.

Findings: **8 critical, 31 wrong, 25 unclear, 1 style**. Critical means a credible unsafe instruction or missing control in the described practical. Wrong means a scientific or cross-file contradiction. Unclear means missing conditions, provenance, approval or model limitations; it does not automatically mean a value is false. Confidence concerns the finding, not approval of the whole activity.

**Release recommendation:** have a chemistry teacher resolve the critical instructions before students use them as real-laboratory guidance. A planned/draft badge and virtual-only environment do not make an unsafe written recipe suitable for practical use. No experiment is approved by the absence of a finding.

Appearance, formula, charge, phase, numerical values actually present, stated quantities, hazards and cross-file agreement were reviewed; absent physical fields were not invented. A correctly rounded formula mass is not evidence that a simulation concentration or safety classification is correct.

Primary chemical/safety/teaching references were checked online; references and retrieval limits are listed below.
